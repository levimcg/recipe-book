  - 450 grams (1 lb.) Ground Beef
  - 450 grams (1 lb.) Ground Pork
  - 2 Eggs
  - 1 Cup Breadcrumbs
  - ¼ Cup Milk
  - 15 grams Tablespoon Garlic Powder
  - 2 Teaspoons Onion Powder
  - 10g Salt
  - 5 grams Black Pepper
  - For the sauce
  - Ketchup
  - Brown Sugar
  - Worchestershire Sauce

## Method

### For the glaze
1. Mix the Ketchup, Brown Sugar, and Worchestershire Sauce together in a bowl
1. Set aside until ready to glaze the meatloaf

### For the meatloaf
1. Combine the remaining ingredients in a large mixing bowl and mix thoroughly to combine.
1. Try not to overwork the mixture, you want to end up with a tender meatloaf, not sausage.
1. Form into a loaf shape and place on a parchment-lined sheet pan.
1. Bake in a 375° oven for 30 minutes.
1. After 30 minutes, take out the meatloaf and brush the the first round of glaze onto the meatloaf.
1. Repeat this glazing process **every 5–10 minutes** until the meatloaf reaches and internal temp of 165°

## Notes
Serve the meatloaf with your favorite mashed potatoes. I like to also serve some steamed broccoli/brocolini along with the mashed potatoes.
