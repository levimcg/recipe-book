title: Ranch Dip
description: We make this super simple dip for almost every cookout. Goes great with a veggie tray or potato chips.
icon: 🥗
prep: 5 min
cook: n/a
ingredients:
  - 32 oz. container sour cream
  - ¼ cup ranch seasoning mix
  - 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  - 1/2 teaspoon MSG
tags:
    - Dips
    - Appetizers
    - Vegetarian
---
## Method
Combine all the ingredients in a large bowl and mix thoroughly to combine.

## Notes
- Add more ranch seasoning powder and garlic powder to taste.
- Save the sour cream container to store the dip.
